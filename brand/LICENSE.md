## What you may do without asking

- Use the marks **to refer to Kitsune** — in an article, a talk, a tutorial, a comparison, a "built for Kitsune" badge on your plugin or theme.
- Reproduce them unmodified, at legible size, with the clear space described in [`README.md`](README.md).

## What you may not do

- Use the marks as, or as part of, **your own** product name, logo, domain name, or app icon.
- Modify, recolour, redraw, or animate them.
- Use them in a way that suggests your work is official, endorsed, or affiliated.
- Ship them as the default logo of an install. A Kitsune site is not branded Kitsune by default; the operator's own logo goes in `site_group.settings.logo`.

You may fork the code — the MPL guarantees it and GOVERNANCE.md calls that a feature. You may not call the fork Kitsune or dress it in these files. Fork freely; pick your own name.
